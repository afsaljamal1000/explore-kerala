Alleppey Backwaters & Houseboats

The Alleppey Backwaters, often called the "Venice of the East," are a breathtaking network of interconnected canals, rivers, and tranquil lakes in Kerala, India. Cruising through these emerald waters on a traditional houseboat (known locally as a kettuvallam) offers an intimate glimpse into rural coastal life, where you can watch palm-fringed villages, vast paddy fields, and local fishermen glide by. These floating homes range from charming, rustic standard models to lavish luxury vessels equipped with air-conditioned bedrooms, private chefs serving authentic Kerala cuisine, and open-air decks. Whether you choose a peaceful day cruise or an overnight stay anchored under the stars, drifting through Alleppey’s serene lagoons provides a uniquely calming escape from the bustle of modern life.
