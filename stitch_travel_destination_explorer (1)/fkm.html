Fort Kochi & Mattancherry

Fort Kochi and Mattancherry are twin historic neighborhoods in Kochi, Kerala, that together form a captivating living museum of colonial history, diverse cultures, and vibrant coastal life. Stepping into Fort Kochi feels like entering a time capsule, where the iconic, shore-bound Chinese Fishing Nets frame dramatic Arabian Sea sunsets, and tree-lined avenues are flanked by Dutch, Portuguese, and British colonial bungalows, the historic St. Francis Church, and creative cafes. A short distance away, Mattancherry shifts the atmosphere to a bustling cultural melting pot, anchored by the fragrant spice markets of Jew Town, the historic Paradesi Synagogue, and the Mattancherry Palace (Dutch Palace) with its stunning traditional Keralan murals. Seamlessly woven together, these neighborhoods serve as the heart of the Kochi-Muziris Biennale, offering a sensory journey through centuries of global trade, artistic expression, and seaside charm.
