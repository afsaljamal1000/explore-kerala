Munnar Hills & Eravikulam

Munnar Hill Station and Eravikulam National Park form the crown jewel of Kerala's Western Ghats, offering a breathtaking blend of rolling tea plantations, mist-shrouded peaks, and rare wildlife. Munnar captivates visitors with its vast, emerald-green tea estates, cascading waterfalls, and cool, refreshing climate that provides a perfect escape from the plains. Just a short drive away, Eravikulam National Park protects the region's pristine high-altitude shola-grassland ecosystem and serves as the primary sanctuary for the endangered Nilgiri tahr, a mountain goat frequently spotted grazing along the park's scenic trekking trails. Together, they create an enchanting destination where you can wander through aromatic spice gardens, witness the dramatic views from Anamudi Peak, and immerse yourself in the tranquil beauty of South India's premier hill country.
